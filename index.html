Privacy

ScienceLens collects only assessment responses and anonymised analytics. Schools remain the data controllers. Student PII is encrypted, never sold, and deletable on request. Reports are role-restricted. We comply with India’s DPDP Act and follow FERPA-style safeguards. The full policy is shared during onboarding.

Terms

The platform is licensed to schools for internal academic use. Assessments may not be redistributed. Service includes monthly assessments, dashboards and support. The pilot is free for 2 cycles; continued use is via annual subscription. Uptime, data retention and support SLAs are detailed in the MSA.
